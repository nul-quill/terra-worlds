    Same seed, same seed-phrase, same world on any machine. Everything is computed on the CPU from a hash of the seed string — no images, no fonts, no libraries. Keys: Space rerolls, arrows walk a cell, Shift+arrows walk the relief bins, 1-9 pick a palette, C cycles channels, H cycles shapes, P steps through the seed phrases, G and L toggle grain and lines, S saves the PNG.
